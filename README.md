# Capstone_2

#Capstone 2 - Sales Analysis 
 
# --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The project analyzes sales data for the company EmporiUm, the goal is to evaluate business perfomance for the assigned territories. 
During the analysis the languages used will be Python. The project will review trends in revenue, customer behaviors, and product performance.

The analysis is done in Jupyter Notebook and will include data cleaning, merging of datasets, aggregation, visualization, and business insights. 

The main goal of the project: 
- Analyze sales performance for Connecticut and New York
- Identify the top-performing stores and customer
- Evaluate the revenue and trends over time
- Overview of the product category performance
- Providing marketing recommendations based on data finds


# -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The project answers several business questions, including:

- Who are the territory managers and which stores are in each territory?
- What is the total monthly revenue for each territory?
- Which stores perform the best and worst?
- Who are the top customers by total spend?
- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- What trends exist in transactions and sales over time?
